From the Field to the Chart. In Real Time.

How Vorro’s BridgeGate™ data orchestration platform and Inovytec’s connected critical-care devices combine into one complete data path — from a roadside SALI station or a Ventway Sparrow ventilator straight into the hospital record.

A Virtual Hospital in the Field Still Needs to Reach the Real One.

Inovytec’s SALI stations and Ventway Sparrow ventilators put critical-care response in the field and stay connected to dispatch in real time. What happens after that connection — getting vitals, ventilation data, and case history into the receiving hospital’s EHR, EMS record, or dispatch platform — is exactly the gap Vorro’s BridgeGate™ EiPaaS closes, translating field data into the HL7/FHIR standards hospitals already run on.
