The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why AdventHealth Colorado, why now

AdventHealth Colorado just finished the hard part: five hospitals, one Epic instance, after a $660 million enterprise rollout. What Epic can't do on its own is reach everything outside it — the independent clinic that faxes, the outside lab, the payer portal, the transition of care that arrives as a PDF. Vorro (BridgeGate EiPaaS) sits underneath as one managed hub: HL7, ADT, FHIR, C-CDA, DICOM and X12 in; governed, monitored, auditable flows into the chart. A new referring practice, payer or partner becomes a configuration, not a project.

The highest-leverage moment is now, right after go-live, while the org is already deep in Epic workflow change — and while ambient AI is ready to take documentation off the clinician on top of that same connected record.

Not a competing product; the plumbing that makes every outside clinic, lab, payer and partner a connector, not a fax. The medicine stays AdventHealth's; any-to-any movement of the data is Vorro.
