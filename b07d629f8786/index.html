The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

Equity Estates manages property operations, travel coordination, and investor reporting across a growing portfolio of vacation homes and fund vehicles, each fund and destination bringing its own local vendor and booking systems into the mix.

Even where each fund's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same investor and property record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what an alternative investment fund Equity Estates' size spends on technology.
