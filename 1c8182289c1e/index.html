The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

The Commonwealth of Pennsylvania's Office for Information Technology runs shared hardware, network, application, and security services across nearly 50 state agencies, each agency bringing its own legacy systems into the enterprise IT environment.

Even where each agency's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same citizen and service record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations for a state government managing IT across nearly 50 agencies.
