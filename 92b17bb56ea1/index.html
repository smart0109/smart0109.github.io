Why Vorro, why now

i-Genie.ai synthesizes billions of searches, social posts, and reviews into consumer insights for brand clients worldwide, each client engagement and new regional market bringing its own data source integrations into the platform.

Even where each data source's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same brand and insight record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a consumer insights company i-Genie's size spends on technology.
