Why Vorro, why now

Cutsforth runs sensor monitoring, plant asset management, and engineering systems for power generation clients, each plant bringing its own SCADA and asset management systems into the deployment.

Even where each plant's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same asset and monitoring record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what an industrial technology company Cutsforth's size spends on technology.
