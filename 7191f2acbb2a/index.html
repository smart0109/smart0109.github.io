The Integration Pitch

Turning patient-carried PDFs into physician-ready data

Prenuvo has built one of the fastest-scaling preventive-imaging networks in the country — 110,000+ members, 80+ radiologists, and a widening web of longevity-care partners like Hone Health, EHE Health, and Parsley Health all feeding into a single scan-and-biomarker platform. The clinical output is genuinely rich: DICOM-grade whole-body imaging, FDA-cleared AI composition and brain-health analysis, and physician-friendly PDF reports. Today, getting that data into a member's actual care team still runs largely through the member themselves — downloading a report, forwarding a DICOM file, or booking a Results Review call.

The highest-leverage moment is now, while Prenuvo is standing up 15 new clinics and its first international sites at once. Every clinic that opens and every longevity partner that goes live multiplies the number of downstream EHRs — Epic, Oracle Health, athenahealth, and the long tail of independent practices — that a Prenuvo report needs to land in without the patient acting as the courier.*

Vorro's role is the connective layer underneath that growth: structured FHIR/HL7/C-CDA exchange that lets Prenuvo push imaging summaries and biomarker results directly into a referring physician's chart, ambient documentation support for the Results Review conversation itself, and a single interoperability surface that scales with Prenuvo's clinic count instead of adding a new manual handoff for each new market Dr. Durand's team opens.
