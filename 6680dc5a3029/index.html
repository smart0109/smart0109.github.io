The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why CHRISTUS Shreveport-Bossier, why now

CHRISTUS owns the hard part: delivering emergency, cardiac, cancer, obstetric and orthopaedic care to Shreveport-Bossier around the clock, on Epic, with CareLink already reaching community providers. What Epic cannot do on its own is reach everything outside it — the independent clinic that faxes, the outside lab, the payer portal, the transition of care that arrives as a PDF. Vorro (BridgeGate EiPaaS) sits underneath as one managed hub: HL7, ADT, FHIR, C-CDA, DICOM and X12 in; governed, monitored, auditable flows into the chart. A new referring practice, payer or partner becomes a configuration, not a project.

The highest-leverage moment is now, while ambient AI is ready to take documentation off the clinician — and the connected record underneath it is what decides whether that works.

Not a competing product; the plumbing that makes every outside clinic, lab, payer and partner a connector, not a fax. The medicine stays CHRISTUS's; any-to-any movement of the data is Vorro.
