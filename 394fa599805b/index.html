The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

OrthoSC already runs athenahealth for records and scheduling, Radix Health for online booking and CareSense for remote patient engagement across its five, soon six, locations and two surgery centers, alongside in-house imaging and physical therapy at every site.

Even where the core systems look consistent across locations, Vorro's job is making sure every site's imaging, therapy and surgery-center systems agree with them too.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain time and budget from staff, streamlining operations across every OrthoSC location.
