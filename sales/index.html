High confidence alone is $245,568, which clears the $225,000 goal by $20,568 — and leaves the $450,000 extension goal $204,432 short. The running total passes $225,000 during October and does not reach $450,000 until December.

December is $300,750 of Arista licence in the low band, with only its $50,000 install committed. Take Arista out entirely and the year lands at $419,068 — still comfortably past $225,000, but $30,932 short of the extension. Arista still has no written proposal, pricing or contract anywhere.

The base goal needs $56,250 a month. Only October clears that on high confidence alone ($124,970); September is $47,970 and November $39,658. For the extension goal the bar is $112,500 a month, which again only October meets.

The bands. High and low are the CRO’s call, not a formula. Most high-confidence deals sit at 60% or better on the CRM record, but TriageLogic (40%), Boyd Beauty (50%) and CareOne (25%) are committed on judgement, and Arista is held in low despite being the largest deal on the board. Meeting and email counts are shown per deal: meetings from a full sweep of 1,378 calendar events, emails counted as inbound only from the prospect’s own domain.

What is not here. A deal appears only if it has a meeting in the last 120 days or scheduled ahead, or 2+ recent inbound emails. That removed $772,500 of Zoho pipeline, mostly OnCare ($610K, no meetings, no customer email since July 2025). WISHIN and LifePoint were removed by hand. Amounts are the CRM value except Wayne, set to $150,000 by the CRO (the CRM record says $360,000; the 23%-fee-plus-AWS-partner-cash calculation gave $225,620), and six accounts priced by assumption because no figure exists anywhere: Avita, DMEscripts, Koning, CareOne, VeeHealthTek, Open_C. Percentages are the probability on each record. The $7,970 monthly line is a placeholder, not a contracted bill: MedRevIQ’s $6,250 was a single invoice request in August, not a confirmed recurring charge, and only Therap’s $1,720 is explicitly recurring. Treat it as an existing-account run rate until finance confirms it.
