The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

Premier runs two core business lines, Supply Chain Services and Performance Services, plus legacy units like Contigo Health that the company is actively winding down, each with its own contracting, analytics and provider data.

Even where each business line's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same provider and contract record as ownership changes hands.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement left behind by a corporate carve-out, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a company Premier's size spends on technology.
