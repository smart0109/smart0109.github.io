The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why Frontier Direct Care, why now

Frontier Direct Care has grown its employer membership base more than 10x in two years: 100+ employers now routing care through an integrated direct primary care, virtual care and referral platform, backed by a fresh $20M Series B.

But every new employer brings its own benefits and HRIS platform, and every referral or direct-contracted specialist and facility runs its own EHR, each one its own connection for the care team to manage.

That's where Vorro's pre-built EHR and benefits-platform connectors help: giving Frontier's clinical and referral network a standard way to exchange data with every employer and specialist, instead of a custom build for each of the 100+ (and growing) employer relationships.
