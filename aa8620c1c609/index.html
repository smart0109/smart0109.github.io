The Integration Pitch

Stop building EHR interfaces one health system at a time

Right now, every new health system InpharmD onboards means another round of point-to-point integration work — mapping Epic's APIs (and whatever the next customer runs, if it isn't Epic) into a bespoke pipe that has to be built, tested, and kept alive against every vendor upgrade. That's engineering time spent on plumbing, not on Sherlock or the product roadmap.

Vorro sits underneath as a single interoperability layer — one FHIR/HL7/C-CDA connection standardizes the data coming out of Epic, Oracle Health, MEDITECH, or whatever EHR the next waitlist conversion runs, instead of a new custom build per customer.

For a small engineering team scaling fast, that's the difference between integration work that grows with your headcount and integration work that grows with your customer count — and only one of those scales.
