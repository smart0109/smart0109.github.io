The Integration Pitch

The Next Mile for QuadMed's Epic Investment

QuadMed has spent since 2019 building one of the deepest employer-sponsored Epic deployments in the country — a single instance spanning onsite, near-site, shared-site, and virtual health centers, reinforced this August by an Epic Gold Stars score in the top 1% of usage worldwide. That kind of depth doesn't happen by accident: it takes exactly the coordination between IT, clinical, and operations teams that Dr. Bustle's Quadruple Aim mandate — better experience, better outcomes, lower cost, engaged providers — is built to drive.

The highest-leverage moment is right after a top-1% Epic score, not before it. QuadMed's own record is excellent; the harder problem is everything that record has to talk to outside the four walls of a QuadMed center — the specialist a patient is referred to, the occupational-health and workers' comp partners tied to employer clients, the TPAs and self-funded employer plans adjudicating claims, and now a brand-new coalition site in Sheboygan standing up fresh connections from scratch. Vorro's interoperability layer sits exactly there: turning QuadMed's Epic data into clean FHIR, HL7, and C-CDA flows to every referring and payer system its employer clients already touch, without adding integration work to an already-stretched IT team.

For a physician-CMO whose mandate is explicitly the Quadruple Aim, the pitch is direct: Vorro extends the same 'depth of use' QuadMed just won recognition for out past Epic's walls — faster referral loops, cleaner prior auth and eligibility checks for employer plans, and ambient documentation that gives clinicians time back — so every new coalition health center QuadMed opens starts fully connected on day one instead of rebuilding integrations market by market.
