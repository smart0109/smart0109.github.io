The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why a global bank, why now

A global bank's hardest problem isn't any one core — it's that every country runs its own regulated environment and the data can't simply be centralized. Vorro (BridgeGate EiPaaS) sits across them as one streaming fabric: ISO 20022, SWIFT, ISO 8583, REST and Kafka in; governed, monitored flows out — into each country's own warehouse, or passed straight through with nothing stored. A new country, core or rail becomes a configuration, not a re-platforming.

The highest-leverage moment is now, with ISO 20022, real-time payments and data-sovereignty all landing together across every environment.

Not a competing product; the fabric that makes every country environment, core and payment rail a connector, not a project — with data kept resident by design. The banking stays the bank's; any-to-any movement of the data is Vorro.

How Vorro moves the data

Streaming integration — your data, your choice of where it lives

Vorro doesn't run nightly batch jobs. Every change is captured and streamed in real time — change-data-capture plus event streaming — across each country's sovereign environment and payment rails. What happens to the data in flight is your decision, and you can choose differently for each environment:

Option A · Land it in your warehouse

Vorro streams normalized, governed data into a data warehouse you own and control — your cloud account, your region, your keys. Vorro maps, validates, and monitors every flow; you keep the single source of truth and can run analytics and AI on it directly.

CDC → your warehouse · you own the store · governed & monitored
Option B · Pass it through, store nothing

Vorro brokers data system-to-system in real time and persists no copy. Records pass back and forth through the fabric; nothing lands at rest in Vorro. Ideal where data-residency or sovereignty law forbids a central copy — the data never leaves its lane.

real-time broker · zero persistence · residency-safe
source systems  →  Vorro stream ( map · validate · monitor )  →  your warehouse  and/or  straight to target  — per environment
Same fabric either way. Streaming, governed, monitored and fully auditable — whether the data lands in your warehouse or only passes through. You decide per environment, and Vorro never locks the data in: it's always yours.
