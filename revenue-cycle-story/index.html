A Vorro story

A hospital was earning the revenue. Its systems just couldn’t get a clean claim out the door.

Same patients, same care delivered. The money was already earned. It was getting stuck between the systems that were supposed to collect it. So we connected them, and went and got what had slipped away.

The challenge

Earned revenue was leaking between disconnected systems

The EHR, the practice-management system, the clearinghouse, the payers, and the patient-billing tools all lived in their own worlds. Eligibility went unchecked, coding and charge data did not flow, and claims went out with gaps. So claims came back denied, AR days climbed, and staff spent their days re-keying and reworking instead of collecting. The hospital was not under-earning; it was under-collecting.

“We were doing the work and delivering the care. We just weren’t getting paid for all of it.”

What we did

We connected the revenue cycle, then recovered what had slipped away

01 · We connected the revenue cycle

One layer, from eligibility to remit

We wired the EHR, practice management, clearinghouse, payers and patient-billing systems into a single managed layer. Eligibility is checked up front, charges and codes flow without re-keying, and clean claims go out the first time. When denials do happen, the data to work them is already in one place.

02 · We recovered what had slipped away

Denials, underpayments and aged AR, worked back to cash

We did not stop at the going-forward fix. Working with our partners, we went after the denials, underpayments and aged receivables that had been written off or forgotten, and turned stuck revenue back into cash the hospital had already earned.
