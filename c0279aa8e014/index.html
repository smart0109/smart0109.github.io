Every Test Result. Every System Connected.

How Vorro’s BridgeGate™ data orchestration platform and UnifAI Health’s connected diagnostics platform combine into one complete, compliant path — from a lateral-flow test or an infection-monitoring alert to the hospital record.

A Connected Test Still Needs a Connected Chart.

UnifAI Health’s AI-powered Digital Reader turns lateral-flow test strips into digital results, and its microbiome monitoring flags harmful bacteria in hospitals and care homes before outbreaks spread. Both are described as “connected” devices — but neither publishes a path into a US hospital’s EHR, lab system, or public-health reporting pipeline. Vorro’s BridgeGate™ EiPaaS is that path: an HL7/FHIR‑fluent, HIPAA‑ready, SOC 2‑compliant layer that carries UnifAI’s device data into the systems US healthcare buyers already require.
