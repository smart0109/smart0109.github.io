The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why Prenuvo, why now

Prenuvo has done something rare in imaging: a vertically integrated network of 26 clinics running its own AI-driven whole-body MRI platform, now converting into real payer and employer relationships: EHE Health, John Hancock, and a growing membership product.

But turning a cash-pay scan into a covered benefit means Prenuvo's imaging and patient systems now need to talk to insurer eligibility systems, employer benefits platforms, and referring physicians' EHRs, each one a different interface.

That's exactly the layer Vorro adds: pre-built connectors across payer, EHR and HIE systems so each new insurance or employer partnership Dr. Durand's team signs is a connector, not a bespoke integration, critical as Prenuvo scales past 26 clinics and into the insured population.
