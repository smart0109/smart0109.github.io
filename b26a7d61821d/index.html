The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

Gameday runs more than 100 company-owned and franchise clinics nationwide plus its first international location in Ontario, each with its own scheduling, EMR and billing setup even though they share the same brand.

Even where each clinic's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same patient and revenue record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a multi-site franchise network Gameday's size spends on technology.
