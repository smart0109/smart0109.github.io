The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why Unico Hospitals, why now

Unico runs a 250-bed multi-specialty tertiary hospital in Dhaka and just signed an MOU with Milvik Bangladesh to build technology-driven healthcare solutions together.

But turning that MOU into a working product means Unico's hospital EHR and Milvik's digital health platform need to exchange real patient and claims data, not just a partnership announcement.

That's where Vorro's pre-built EHR and HIE connectors help: giving Moinul's team a fast, standard way to wire the hospital's EHR into Milvik's platform and any future digital-health partner, instead of building each integration from scratch.
