The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

RiverWoods runs three campuses across Exeter, Durham and Manchester, each spanning independent living, assisted living, memory care and skilled nursing with its own resident, health and billing records.

Even where each campus's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same resident and care record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a CCRC RiverWoods' size spends on technology.
