The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

PCHS runs a critical access hospital, medical clinic, emergency department, surgery and rehabilitation services, each with its own scheduling, clinical and billing systems supporting a rural, county-wide population.

Even where each department's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same patient and revenue record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a critical access hospital PCHS's size spends on technology.
