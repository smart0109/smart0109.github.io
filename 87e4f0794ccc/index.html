Why Vorro, why now

Forbes Technology Council runs membership vetting, content publishing, and event systems for a global community of technology executives, each program bringing its own registration and content systems into the mix.

Even where each program's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same member record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a membership organization Forbes Technology Council's size spends on technology.
