The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

RestorixHealth operates 236 outpatient wound centers across 280+ partner hospitals plus mobile and in-home services, each partner facility bringing its own EHR and scheduling systems into the wound care workflow.

Even where each partner hospital's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same patient and wound record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a wound care company RestorixHealth's size spends on technology.
