The Integration Pitch

For Olaf Schwartz — the integration layer under 100+ sites

amedes operates a federated diagnostics network — 100+ lab and clinical sites, thousands of referring practices, and its own LIS/automation infrastructure — where each new practice connection or site consolidation traditionally means another bespoke HL7 or GDT interface for your team to build, test, and keep alive. That's engineering time spent on data plumbing between disparate practice systems and your labs, not on the automation and platform work that actually differentiates amedes' turnaround times.

Vorro is an integration engine purpose-built to sit under exactly this kind of many-to-many healthcare data topology: normalize inbound HL7v2, FHIR, C-CDA and GDT-style feeds from referring practices into one consistent internal model, expose clean FHIR/REST APIs and webhooks outward, and absorb the interface sprawl that comes with every new site, practice, or lab merger — instead of a growing pile of point-to-point mappings your team owns forever.

For a CIO managing integration across a multi-country group under GDPR, that means fewer brittle one-off feeds, faster onboarding of new referring practices and consolidated sites, and an API surface your engineers actually want to build on — freeing them to work on the diagnostics platform itself rather than re-plumbing every time the network grows.
