Why Vorro, why now

ELLKAY connects hospitals, labs, payers and IT vendors across 750+ EHR and practice management systems, each client engagement bringing its own combination of source and destination systems into a migration or interoperability project.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what an interoperability company ELLKAY's size spends on technology.
