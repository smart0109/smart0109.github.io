Why Vorro, why Solid Biosciences, why now

Solid Biosciences is scaling fast: a next-generation capsid platform, a new multi-omic analytics partnership with NanoMosaic, and its largest-ever scientific presence at ASGCT 2026: real momentum toward commercial-scale gene therapy manufacturing.

But every new partner, instrument and analytics platform is another system that has to talk to manufacturing, quality and regulatory (LIMS, MES, QMS, CTMS) and now Tessie's multi-omic output all need to move data cleanly as Holly Springs scales toward commercial production.

That's exactly where Vorro fits: pre-built connectors across the LIMS/MES/QMS/CTMS stack so a new analytics partner, instrument or CDMO relationship is a connector, not a months-long integration project, freeing Paul's team to focus on getting material to patients, not pipe-fitting data.
