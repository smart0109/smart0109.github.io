The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

FQHC 340B Compliance monitors 340B eligibility, referral capture and pharmacy claims for FQHC clients nationwide, each health center bringing its own EHR, e-prescribing and pharmacy claims systems into the audit relationship.

Even where each health center's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same 340B eligibility and claims record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a 340B compliance firm's size spends on technology.
