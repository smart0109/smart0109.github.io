The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

IF MedTech ships connected diagnostics and monitoring devices; every hospital customer will ask how that data reaches their EHR and LIS. Vorro is a single healthcare integration platform that connects devices, applications, and clinical systems over HL7, FHIR, X12, and APIs. One layer serves the whole portfolio, so interoperability becomes a standard product answer instead of a per-deal engineering project. Your embedded and software teams stay focused on devices while Vorro manages transformation, routing, and monitoring. Start with one device line and one customer feed, prove it live, then extend across the catalog.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what an organization your size spends on IT.
