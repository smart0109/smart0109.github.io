Where Vorro fits

GNAA's mission is nurses confidently shaping how AI gets used in healthcare — but AI is only as trustworthy as the data underneath it. Across GNAA's member health systems, that data still lives in siloed EHRs, lab feeds, and point-to-point interfaces. Vorro is the integration layer that turns those fragmented systems into one clean, standards-based feed so the clinical decision-support tools GNAA's Health Informatics Division is exploring can actually be trusted at the bedside.

Acronyms, in plain English

The alphabet soup this brief uses, decoded.

EHR/EMR Electronic Health Record / Medical Record — a hospital's core clinical data system.
HL7 Health Level 7 — the long-standing messaging standard for exchanging clinical data between systems.
FHIR Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources — the modern, API-based standard for sharing clinical data.
CDS Clinical Decision Support — tools (increasingly AI-enabled) that help clinicians make care decisions.
