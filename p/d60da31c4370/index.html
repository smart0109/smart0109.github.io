The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

Auxilio Mutuo is mid-flight on an Oracle Health EHR and RevElate rollout, and every ancillary, lab, and payer connection has to be rebuilt or revalidated. Vorro is a healthcare integration platform that speaks HL7 v2, FHIR, and X12 natively and sits between Oracle Health and everything else as one managed layer. Instead of dozens of point-to-point interfaces, your team monitors one hub with visibility into every message that moves. That means faster cutover, fewer stalled claims, and clinical data arriving in real time across a 600-bed operation. We can start with one high-pain interface, prove it live, and expand from there.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what an organization your size spends on IT.
