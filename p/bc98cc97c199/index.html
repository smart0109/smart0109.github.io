35M+ people supported yearly

ALA reaches more than 35 million people living with chronic lung disease and 44 million with acute respiratory illness each year.

Data-driven by design

Lung HelpLine, State of the Air, State of Lung Cancer, and State of Tobacco Control all run on program and research data moving cleanly across systems.

Who they are

The American Lung Association is one of the oldest voluntary health organizations in the U.S. — founded in 1904 to fight tuberculosis, today focused on defeating lung cancer, championing clean air, improving quality of life for people with lung disease, and a tobacco-free future. It runs national mission programs (Lung HelpLine, Freedom From Smoking, LUNG FORCE, Better Breathers Clubs) alongside research, advocacy, and donor engagement — each with its own systems and data.

Where Vorro fits in the ALA stack

ALA runs mission programs — HelpLine intake, screening navigation, tobacco cessation, research grants — alongside donor and marketing systems that all need to stay in sync. Vorro is the integration layer connecting them, so a change in one system shows up correctly everywhere else, without a manual reconciliation project every quarter.

The shape of the integration hasn't changed quarter to quarter — what's flowed through it has grown as new programs came online.

Why this keeps paying off

  • One integration layer means HelpLine, program, and donor systems stay in sync without a rebuild every time ALA launches something new.
  • Less manual reconciliation between CRM, marketing, and program data — more staff time on mission work.
  • Clean, governed data underpins State of the Air, State of Lung Cancer, State of Tobacco Control, and funder/board reporting.
  • The architecture scales with ALA's program footprint — new HelpLine channels, screening navigators, or research grants plug into what's already connected.
