Who they are

Koning Health (Norcross, GA) builds the Koning Vera Breast CT, a dedicated compression-free 3D imaging system for breast tissue — already installed across the U.S. and internationally in Thailand, Qatar, the Netherlands, and (per recent regulatory clearance) the UAE. Scans move through a distributed reading network: Innovative TeleRadiology (innotelerad.com) is Koning's distributor and 24/7/365 teleradiology reading partner for the device. Dr. David Bodne — Koning Health's Co-Chief Medical Officer and a 30-year breast-imaging radiologist — practices through that same reading network, sitting at the intersection of the device maker and the clinical read.

Angles for the conversation

Three ways to frame the conversation given where the implementation stands today.

1

Every new install is a new integration point

Seattle and Richmond just came online after Jamaica, California, and Texas. Each site brings its own local PACS/RIS — the question worth putting on the table is whether onboarding stays a repeatable data pattern or turns into a custom project every time.

2

The read happens outside the device

Innovative TeleRadiology's distributed, 24/7/365 model means the scan, the read, and the referring provider are never in the same system. That handoff — not the scanner itself — is the integration surface worth reviewing.

3

Regulatory certification raises the bar on the data side too

MDSAP (Aug 2026) is a quality-infrastructure milestone aimed at Health Canada and TGA submissions. As the device's regulatory footprint goes international, the data plumbing behind it should be built to the same standard.

Why this, why now

  • Koning Health is actively expanding — Seattle and Richmond went live in August 2026, on top of prior Jamaica, California, and Texas installs — and each new site is a fresh integration point.
  • MDSAP certification just opened the door to Health Canada and Australia's TGA, meaning the data layer needs to be ready to scale internationally alongside the device.
  • Every scan already depends on a clean handoff between the Koning Vera device, the Innovative TeleRadiology read, and the referring provider's EHR — that is exactly the kind of healthcare data movement Vorro is built for.
  • Dr. Bodne sits at the intersection of both organizations — Koning Health's clinical leadership and Innovative TeleRadiology's reading practice — which makes this the right room to align the data roadmap with the growth plan.

Acronyms, in plain English

The alphabet soup this brief uses, decoded.

KBCT Koning (Vera) Breast CT — the company's dedicated breast computed-tomography scanner.
MDSAP Medical Device Single Audit Program — one regulatory audit recognized by the US, Canada, Australia, and other member countries.
BI-RADS Breast Imaging Reporting and Data System — the standardized scale radiologists use to report breast imaging findings.
PACS / RIS Picture Archiving and Communication System / Radiology Information System — where images and orders live at each site.
DICOM Digital Imaging and Communications in Medicine — the standard format imaging devices use to emit images and study data.
