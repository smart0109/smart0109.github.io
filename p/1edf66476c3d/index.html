The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

Your cancer program only works when the oncology EHR, the radiation-oncology system, labs, pathology, imaging, and the payer and prior-auth side all agree on the same patient. When they do not, treatment stalls and staff reconcile it by hand. Vorro is the layer that keeps every one of these systems in sync so care does not wait on data. The integration is any to any, HL7 translates to FHIR in both directions, and nothing in the existing stack has to be replaced.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what an organization your size spends on IT.
