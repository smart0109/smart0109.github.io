Vorro × YewMaker · Sustainable medicines, wired in

Getting MCF carbon-footprint data to where medicines are actually prescribed

YewMaker turns the environmental impact of medicines into hard numbers. Vorro is the layer that can carry those numbers, live, into the EHR, the formulary, and NHS net-zero reporting — so a greener choice shows up at the moment of prescribing, not in a separate spreadsheet.

Who YewMaker is
Partnerships
The Sustainable Medicines Partnership (SMP): a 48-organisation action collaborative that ran a four-year programme to cut medicine waste.
Products
MCF Classifier: Medicine Carbon Footprint ratings and CO2e values via MCF Formulary and MCF Data, covering thousands of products and ingredients.
Thought leadership
Research, playbooks, frameworks and events driving sustainable-healthcare policy, including the "Every Dose Used" playbook.
Recent: two new MCF datasets (CO2e for 1000s of products/ingredients), the SMP Repository (30 programme outputs), and the "Every Dose Used" playbook.Affiliations: IQVIA; coverage tied to NHS net-zero goals.
Where Vorro fits
From dataset to live signal
Today MCF ships as downloadable datasets and a standalone formulary. Vorro can wrap MCF as an interface and route CO2e values into the systems clinicians and ICS teams already use — turning a reference dataset into an embedded, real-time signal.

Why talk to Vorro

Vorro is the plumbing between good data and the point of care

YewMaker has done the hard science: standardized, stratified carbon footprints for medicines at scale. The open question is distribution — how those values reach the EHR, the e-prescribing step, the hospital and ICS formulary, and net-zero reporting without every partner building a bespoke integration. That is exactly the layer Vorro provides: one live integration that maps, translates and syncs a data source into the systems that consume it.

Because Haroon built MCF as a data-native model, the most useful first session is technical — access patterns (API vs dataset), terminology and FHIR/formulary mapping, and where a CO2e value should surface in the prescribing workflow.
