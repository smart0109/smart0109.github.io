The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

Lindenberg Cancer & Hematology Center's cancer program only works when the oncology EHR, radiation-oncology system, labs, pathology, imaging, and the payer/prior-auth side agree on the same patient. When they don't, treatment stalls and staff reconcile it by hand. Vorro is the layer that keeps every one of these systems in sync so care doesn't wait on data.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what an organization your size spends on IT.
