The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

American Addiction Centers runs residential and outpatient facilities across multiple states plus a dedicated veterans program, each with its own admissions, clinical and billing systems.

Even where each facility's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same patient and revenue record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a multi-facility treatment network AAC's size spends on technology.
