The Integration Pitch

Connecting MBST to Every Partner Record

MedTec's MBST platform generates a rich, structured stream of treatment and outcomes data at every session — but that data is only as useful as the ease with which it reaches the referring physician's EHR, the sports-medicine team's record, or the next clinic in a patient's care journey. Today, much of that reconciliation happens by fax, PDF, or manual re-entry across dozens of partner sites and several countries.

The highest-leverage moment is now, while MBST is actively formalizing structured research partnerships and expanding into new markets. Vorro's interoperability layer can sit between the MBST device platform and each partner's Epic, Oracle Health, athenahealth or regional record system — mapping session notes, device telemetry, and outcome measures into standard FHIR and HL7 exchanges automatically, in the partner's own language and format.

For Dr. Schreiner's team, that means clinical evidence and outcomes data flow back from every partner clinic and sports program without manual reconciliation, referral letters arrive structured instead of scanned, and MBST's expanding international footprint scales without each new market adding its own integration burden.
