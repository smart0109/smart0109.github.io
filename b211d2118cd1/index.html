The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

MUSC Children's Health runs Epic as its system-wide record, and its new pediatric partnership with Roper St. Francis Healthcare now means two health systems must keep pharmacy, orders and records working together for the same children.

Even where the core EHR looks unified on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every partner system, lab and pharmacy interface agrees with it too.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain time and budget from clinical teams across a health system MUSC's size.
