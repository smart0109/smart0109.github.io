The Integration Pitch

One Integration Layer for Every Client EHR

Smarter Technologies was built in 2025 by combining Access Healthcare, SmarterDx, and Thoughtful.ai, then adding Ruben Amarasingham's Pieces Technologies that September to launch SmarterNotes — clinical AI that writes inside the EHR and revenue-cycle intelligence that watches what it says. The company now touches over 60 hospitals and health systems, 500,000+ providers, and more than $200 billion in managed revenue, with Pieces' documentation engine alone responsible for over 10 million AI-generated clinical notes.

The highest-leverage moment is now: every one of those 60+ hospitals runs its own EHR — Epic here, Oracle Health there, MEDITECH or athenahealth elsewhere — and every new logo Smarter Technologies signs, including the ones SmarterUtilization is chasing this year, means another bespoke interface engineering has to stand up before the AI ever sees a chart. That's exactly the layer Vorro exists to collapse: one FHIR/HL7/C-CDA connection point instead of N one-off builds, so go-lives compress from months to weeks.

Dr. Amarasingham has said AI should work in service of clinicians, not the other way around — but ambient documentation and revenue intelligence only serve clinicians if they reach every chart, on every EHR, on day one of a new health-system contract. Vorro's interoperability layer is the piece that turns Smarter Technologies' clinical-AI-plus-RCM pitch into a plug-and-play deployment story instead of a systems-integration project.
