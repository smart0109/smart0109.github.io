The Integration Pitch

Why BridgeGate, why now

Apex Pharmacy's day runs across the pharmacy management system, the Surescripts e-prescribing feed, the PBM claims switch, and wholesaler EDI — four rails that must line up on every fill or a claim rejects and a patient waits. BridgeGate cleans the flow between them so scripts move, claims adjudicate, and inventory reconciles without the manual chase.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what an organization your size spends on IT.
