The Integration Pitch

An Integration Layer Built for HFH's Next Phase of Growth

Hanoi French Hospital has spent the last few years scaling deliberately — beds up from 68 to 170, a new outpatient and emergency building, and now a formal clinical partnership with Viet Duc University Hospital that just delivered Vietnam's first combined heart-liver transplant with domino liver transplantation. As Directeur général, Erwan Debuc is steering a hospital whose growth increasingly depends not just on beds and specialists, but on how fast and reliably clinical data can move between HFH and every partner it works with.

Every new hospital partnership, insurer relationship, or corporate health contract HFH adds comes with its own handoff — a referral summary re-typed by hand, an imaging file emailed as a PDF, a lab result read over the phone. Each one is a point of friction that slows a deal down, frustrates referring physicians at partner institutions, and creates real clinical risk when something gets lost between systems that were never built to talk to each other.

Vorro gives HFH a single integration layer — FHIR, HL7, C-CDA, REST APIs — that connects the hospital's core systems to Viet Duc, Bach Mai, insurers, and future partners without a bespoke project every time, plus ambient documentation tools that cut clinician charting time and free capacity for the specialty growth HFH is chasing. For Debuc, that's not an IT upgrade — it's the infrastructure that lets HFH say yes to the next partnership, the next insurer, and the next specialty program faster than competitors can.
