The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

Brockville General Hospital spent five years bringing six area hospitals onto Lumeo, one shared Oracle Health record across 33 sites, while still coordinating with provincial systems like ConnectingOntario and its own lab, imaging and pharmacy programs.

Even with a shared regional record now live, Vorro's job is making sure every provincial, departmental and partner system keeps agreeing with it.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, taking roughly 35% out of what a community hospital Brockville's size spends on technology.
