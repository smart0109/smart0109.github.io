The Integration Pitch

Bring Shodair's psychiatric and genetics data — and a statewide referral network — into one view

Shodair Children's Hospital carries a dual mission few facilities anywhere combine: it is Montana's only hospital offering both acute and residential psychiatric care for children and adolescents under one roof, and it runs the state's genetics program, sending outreach clinics into communities across Montana. Every referral, consult, and follow-up moves through community mental health centers, schools, courts, crisis departments, and referring pediatricians scattered across a state larger than many countries.

The highest-leverage moment is now — behavioral health documentation carries its own burden, 42 CFR Part 2 consent requirements make data sharing more delicate than standard HIPAA workflows, and a statewide referral footprint means records arrive in every format imaginable, from fax to whatever EHR the sending clinic runs. Vorro sits underneath the record Shodair already uses, turning that inbound chaos into clean, consented, structured data clinicians can trust.

For Dr. Hoffman, that means faster, cleaner intake from referring providers and schools, ambient documentation that gives clinicians time back with patients instead of screens, and a genetics program whose outreach clinics can exchange results with the main campus and with families' other providers without manual reconciliation — all while keeping consent-sensitive behavioral health data governed the way 42 CFR Part 2 demands.
