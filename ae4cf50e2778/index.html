The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

Next Level Medical runs urgent care, primary care and employer health programs across Houston, San Antonio, Austin and Beaumont, each clinic and employer partnership bringing its own scheduling, EHR and payment systems.

Even where each clinic's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same patient record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what an urgent care organization Next Level Medical's size spends on technology.
