Every EEG. Every Hospital. One Connected Read.

How Vorro’s BridgeGate™ data orchestration platform and Wisemen Neurosciences’ remote neurology and EEG-reading practice combine into one seamless path — from a STAT study at a partner hospital to a structured report back in the chart.

Remote Neurology Runs on Data That Moves as Fast as the Patient Needs It To.

Wisemen Neurosciences reads EEG studies and delivers neurocritical-care consultation for hospitals, health networks, and neurodiagnostic labs that don’t have 24/7 coverage in-house. Every one of those client sites runs its own EHR, its own EEG/lab system, and its own file-transfer habits. Vorro’s BridgeGate™ EiPaaS turns that patchwork into one governed, real-time data path, so a STAT study reaches the reading team, and the finding reaches the chart, at the speed a stroke or seizure call actually demands.
