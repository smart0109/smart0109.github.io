The Integration Pitch

Why BridgeGate, why now

Long Island Plastic Surgical Group runs the practice on a stack that rarely talks: the PM/EHR, before-and-after imaging, POS and patient-financing, plus marketing and CRM all hold a different slice of the same patient. BridgeGate ties them into one record so a consult, a photo, a payment, and a follow-up are the same story — not four systems someone stitches together by hand.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what an organization your size spends on IT.
