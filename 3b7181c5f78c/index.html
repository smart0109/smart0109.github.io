The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

The Children's Institute runs pediatric rehab, behavioral health and early-intervention programs alongside its own licensed Day School, so clinical records, state early-intervention reporting and school records all have to stay in sync for the same child.

Even where each program's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure clinical, therapy and school records all agree on the same child.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, taking roughly 35% out of what a nonprofit the Institute's size spends on technology.
