The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

Shannon just brought a newly acquired hospital and a new 100-bed tower onto its systems, on top of Shannon Clinic's 400-plus providers across 40 specialties and 25 locations, all while running its own telehealth service and a Mayo Clinic Care Network affiliation.

Even with Epic unifying the core medical record, Vorro's job is making sure the newly acquired hospital, every clinic location and outside partners like Mayo all agree on the same patient data.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, taking roughly 35% out of what a health system Shannon's size spends on technology.
