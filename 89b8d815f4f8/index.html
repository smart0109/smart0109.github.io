The Integration Pitch

Stop building interface plumbing per hospital

Every HarmonyCVI or Hospital Records Manager rollout starts the same way: figure out what EHR and PACS the client hospital runs, then hand-build the HL7/FHIR interface to get patient demographics, orders, and imaging results flowing in and results flowing back out. That's engineering time spent on connectivity plumbing instead of on the diagnostic AI and product features that actually differentiate TechVedika's offerings.

Vorro sits underneath as the integration layer, so your team builds to one consistent API instead of re-deriving an interface for every hospital's Epic, Oracle Health, MEDITECH, or CPSI instance. Standard HL7v2, FHIR R4, and C-CDA in; a single normalized feed out to HarmonyCVI, Hospital Records Manager, or whatever you ship next.

That turns interoperability from a recurring services cost on every new deal into a one-time integration, freeing your presales and solutions team to close hospital clients faster and freeing engineering to spend its cycles on the AI models and product roadmap, not on maintaining brittle point-to-point feeds across a growing client base.
