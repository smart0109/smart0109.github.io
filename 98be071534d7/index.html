The Integration Pitch

One Record, Every Client Hospital

Enjoin's physician advisors and CDI specialists don't work inside one record — they work inside dozens, moving between whatever EHR each client health system runs (commonly Epic, Oracle Health, or MEDITECH) to catch documentation gaps before a claim goes out. That cross-record movement is the whole business model: pre-bill chart review, physician queries, and denial defense only pay off if the clinical picture assembles as fast as the coder needs it.

The highest-leverage moment is now. With the December 2025 acquisition of Pediatric Resource Group, Enjoin's team must reconcile MS-DRG and APR-DRG methodologies side by side, across adult and pediatric/NICU documentation, inside an even wider spread of client EHRs — exactly the kind of multi-system, multi-format data pull that turns into query backlog and missed pre-bill windows without a unifying layer underneath it.

Vorro's interoperability platform gives Enjoin's physician advisors and coders one normalized view of clinical documentation, coding, and claims data across every client hospital's record — FHIR, HL7, C-CDA, or a fax-and-PDF holdout — so pre-bill review, physician queries, and denial appeals move at the speed Enjoin already promises its health-system clients, without new integration work every time Enjoin signs a client or a client's EHR changes.
