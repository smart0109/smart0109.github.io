The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro for Florida Heart and Vascular

Dr. Kenigsberg co-founded Florida Heart and Vascular and now leads it as President through a real growth moment: a new flagship Fort Lauderdale facility, expanded imaging capacity, and a referral base spanning primary care physicians and hospital partners across Broward County. Every one of those growth vectors — a new site, a new referring practice, a new hospital relationship — creates another point-to-point data connection that has to be built, secured, and maintained.

That's the hidden cost of growth in cardiology: each new location or referral partner isn't just clinical capacity, it's another integration project competing for the same limited resources. Vorro replaces the accumulating pile of one-off HL7 feeds and manual record requests with a single interoperability layer — one connection point for EHR data, imaging results, referral records, and hospital discharge feeds — so expansion adds patients, not integration debt.

For a practice president, the payoff is strategic, not technical: faster onboarding of new locations and referring physicians, fewer delays in getting imaging and device data to the physicians who need it, and a cleaner compliance posture as TEFCA and information-blocking rules tighten. Vorro also layers in ambient documentation and coding support that gives physicians time back — a direct lever on throughput and growth capacity without adding headcount.
