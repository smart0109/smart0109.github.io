The Integration Pitch

Why WGH Argentina's board should care about interoperability

WGH Argentina's national board — Judit Díaz Bazán among them — governs a mission that depends on visibility across a fragmented landscape: former ministers, hospital CEOs, and medical directors spread across provincial health ministries, universities, and public and private facilities that each run their own records and reporting systems. The board's advocacy for evidence-based policy and gender equity in health leadership is only as strong as the data it can actually compare across that footprint.

That's precisely the gap Vorro closes. Rather than asking member institutions to standardize on one platform, Vorro sits underneath the systems they already run — normalizing HL7, FHIR, and C-CDA feeds from disparate hospital and ministry systems into a single, governable layer the network's leadership can actually see and act on.

For a board whose own vice president co-chairs a regional health-informatics network and coordinates cross-border outbreak surveillance, the stakes of fragmented data aren't abstract — they're the daily reality of coordinating across Argentina's provinces. Vorro gives WGH Argentina's leadership the connective infrastructure to match the ambition of its mission: a network that can see itself clearly enough to lead.
