Why Vorro, why now

Recursion's automated wet lab, AI models and drug pipeline systems run alongside partnerships with Sanofi, Roche and its merged Exscientia chemistry platform, each bringing its own data infrastructure into the workflow.

Even where each lab and partner system looks tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same experimental and compound record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a biopharmaceutical AI company Recursion's size spends on technology.
