The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

PointClickCare's platform connects over 21,000 LTPAC providers, each operator bringing its own pharmacy, hospital referral and billing systems into the shared EHR environment.

Even where each operator's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same resident and clinical record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a senior care EHR company PointClickCare's size spends on technology.
