The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why La Paz, why now

La Paz is Guatemala's largest private hospital group: 7 locations, 115+ specialists and advanced imaging like 1.5T MRI and 128-slice CT, serving patients across multiple insurance companies and uninsured patients alike.

But every insurance company La Paz bills, and every one of its 7 locations, means separate claims and records interfaces to keep straight, exactly the kind of friction that gets in the way of genuinely person-centered care.

That's where Vorro's pre-built EHR and payer connectors help: giving Erick's team a consistent way to move patient and claims data across all 7 La Paz locations and every insurance partner, so continuity of care doesn't depend on which hospital or payer a patient walks into.
