The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

One Million Degrees runs scholar tracking, stipend disbursement, and coaching coordination across seven City Colleges of Chicago campuses and partner colleges, each campus bringing its own enrollment and financial aid systems into the mix.

Even where each campus's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same scholar record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a nonprofit organization One Million Degrees' size spends on technology.
