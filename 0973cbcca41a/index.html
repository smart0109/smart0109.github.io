Why Vorro, why now

Procure Analytics runs consortium buying, dashboard reporting, and analytics systems across 500+ member companies and 12,000 locations, each member bringing its own procurement and ERP systems into the shared platform.

Even where each member's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same spend and supplier record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a group purchasing organization Procure Analytics' size spends on technology.
