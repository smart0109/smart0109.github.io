The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

Datavant links patient records across thousands of provider, payer and life sciences partners, each partner running its own EHR, claims and data warehouse systems that Datavant's tokenization has to reconcile.

Even where each partner's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same tokenized patient record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a data connectivity company Datavant's size spends on technology.
