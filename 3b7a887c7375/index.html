Why Vorro, why now

Shop LC runs live broadcast, reverse auction, and e-commerce systems alongside its own vertically integrated manufacturing operations, each function bringing its own real-time inventory and order systems into the mix.

Even where each system's own tools look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same inventory and order record in real time.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a home shopping network Shop LC's size spends on technology.
