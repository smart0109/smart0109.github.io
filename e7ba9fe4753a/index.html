The Integration Pitch

Turning the James Cancer Network into one connected record

OSUCCC – James runs one of the largest cancer hospitals in the country on Epic, but the James Cancer Network extends its reach through community oncology affiliates across Ohio — Dayton Physicians, Wooster Community Hospital, Memorial Health, and more. Each of those practices manages its own patient records, referral letters, and pathology reports, which today move to Columbus largely as faxes, PDFs, and manual chart pulls. For a role centered on the strategy behind the Center for Cancer Innovation, that gap between affiliate documentation and the James's own record is exactly the kind of friction that slows a patient's path from community diagnosis to comprehensive cancer care.

A tumor board can only be as good as the record in front of it — and right now, that record is often incomplete until someone tracks down the rest by phone or fax.

Vorro connects affiliate and referring-practice systems directly into the James's clinical workflow — FHIR, HL7, and C-CDA pipes that pull outside records, imaging, and pathology into a single view — alongside an ambient documentation layer that captures visit notes, translates in real time, and drafts ICD-10/CPT coding automatically. For a cancer network built on coordination between a flagship hospital and a growing set of community partners, that means faster complete records, fewer administrative handoffs, and more time for the clinical work the Center for Cancer Innovation exists to advance.
