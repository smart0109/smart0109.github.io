The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

HealthTrust runs group purchasing, supply chain and performance improvement systems across 58 exclusive health system partners and HCA's network of 2,000+ sites of care, each site bringing its own procurement and inventory systems.

Even where each member health system's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same supply and contract record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a supply chain company HealthTrust's size spends on technology.
