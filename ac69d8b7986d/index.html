The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why GSK, why now

GSK's hardest problem is that its most valuable data is trapped in worlds that don't speak: OPC-UA and historians on the shop floor, LIMS and EDC in the lab, SAP in supply chain, Veeva in quality and commercial. Vorro (BridgeGate EiPaaS) sits across them as one GxP-validated streaming fabric: OPC-UA, ISA-95, HL7/FHIR, CDISC, IDoc/EDI and Kafka in; governed, auditable flows out — landed in GSK's own validated warehouse, or passed straight through where a copy isn't allowed.

The highest-leverage moment is now, as Pharma 4.0, AI and serialization all demand connected lab-to-shop-floor-to-ERP data under GxP.

Not a competing product; the fabric that makes every site, instrument, MES and ERP a connector, not a project — validated and auditable end to end. The science and the manufacturing stay GSK's; any-to-any movement of the data is Vorro.

How Vorro moves the data

Streaming integration — your data, your choice of where it lives

Vorro doesn't run nightly batch jobs. Every change is captured and streamed in real time — change-data-capture plus event streaming — across the lab, the shop floor, the ERP, quality and the warehouse. What happens to the data in flight is your decision, and you can choose differently for each environment:

Option A · Land it in your warehouse

Vorro streams normalized, governed data into a data warehouse you own and control — your cloud account, your region, your keys. Vorro maps, validates, and monitors every flow; you keep the single source of truth and can run analytics and AI on it directly.

CDC → your warehouse · you own the store · governed & monitored
Option B · Pass it through, store nothing

Vorro brokers data system-to-system in real time and persists no copy. Records pass back and forth through the fabric; nothing lands at rest in Vorro. Ideal where GxP or data-sovereignty rules forbids a central copy — the data never leaves its lane.

real-time broker · zero persistence · residency-safe
source systems  →  Vorro stream ( map · validate · monitor )  →  your warehouse  and/or  straight to target  — per environment
Same fabric either way. Streaming, governed, monitored and fully auditable — whether the data lands in your warehouse or only passes through. You decide per environment, and Vorro never locks the data in: it's always yours.
