Why Vorro, why now

Nsight manages program enrollment, device logistics, patient onboarding and clinical outreach for thousands of partner practices, each practice bringing its own EHR and billing systems into the remote monitoring workflow.

Even where each partner practice's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same patient and vitals record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a remote care company Nsight's size spends on technology.
