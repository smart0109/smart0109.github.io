The Integration Pitch

Keeping one clinical program across a fast-scaling network

As Regional Medical Director, Dr. Eller is accountable for the clinical quality of every patient who walks into a Radial clinic in her region — whether they're starting BrainsWay TMS, Spravato, or PRISM neurofeedback for depression, OCD, or PTSD that hasn't responded to standard care. That accountability runs on paper trails today: referral notes and medication histories that arrive from outside psychiatrists and PCPs as faxes or scanned PDFs, device- and protocol-level session data that lives outside the core chart, and — as of this month — a second intake and eligibility workflow inherited from the newly acquired Mindful Health Solutions clinics.

Vorro exists for exactly this moment. We turn referral records, prior treatment histories, and device or session data into structured, chart-ready information the instant a patient is scheduled — so a clinician opening a first Spravato or TMS consult sees the full clinical picture instead of a stack of unlinked PDFs. Layered on top, ambient documentation captures the visit itself: intake and follow-up conversations become structured notes with ICD-10/CPT coding attached automatically, giving clinicians back the minutes they'd otherwise spend charting between treatment-resistant cases. And because it speaks FHIR, HL7, and C-CDA natively, it plugs into whatever system each newly onboarded clinic — legacy Radial or legacy Mindful Health Solutions — happens to run, without a bespoke integration project for every site.

For a regional program built on treatment protocols and remission tracking, that consistency is the product: every clinic capturing outcomes the same way, every clinician spending visit time on the patient instead of the paperwork, and every new site the network adds — inside this acquisition or the next one — coming online on day one instead of month three.
