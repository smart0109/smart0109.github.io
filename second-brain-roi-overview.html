One-page overview · benefits & ROI

Where Second Brain pays for itself, call by call.

AI Associate sits alongside your revenue team through every stage of a deal — before the call, during it, and in everything that has to happen after — and turns the parts that used to take time into things that already happened.

Meeting Prep

AE

Every call starts already briefed — account history, open items, and the right materials pulled up before anyone dials in.

ROI

No scrambling in the five minutes before a call, and no meeting that opens with “let me find that.”

Follow-Up

AE

The recap and next-step email are drafted the moment the call ends, in the rep’s own voice.

ROI

Follow-ups go out same-day instead of whenever someone gets to it — deals don’t cool while a draft sits unwritten.

Legal Documents

AE · Legal

Drafts contracts, NDAs, and redlines straight from the call itself, using approved templates and language.

ROI

Shrinks the gap between a verbal yes and a signature; legal spends less time writing first drafts from scratch.

Notes & Transcription

AE

Every call is captured and structured automatically, so no one has to choose between listening and typing.

ROI

The record exists whether or not someone remembered to take notes — nothing depends on one person’s memory.

Instant Rebuttals

AE

Surfaces the right proof point, case study, or answer the moment an objection comes up, mid-call.

ROI

Fewer “let me get back to you” moments that give a deal time to lose momentum.

Time to Revenue

Leadership

Removes the paperwork and handoff delays that sit between a good conversation and a closed deal.

ROI

Deals move at the speed of the relationship, not at the speed of whoever’s turn it is to do the admin work.

Leadership Dashboards

Leadership

Rolls up call activity, account status, and rep workload into one live view — no status-update meeting required.

ROI

Leadership sees where deals and reps actually stand without having to ask for it.

Updating CRM Automatically

Both

Account and opportunity records update themselves from what was actually said on the call.

ROI

CRM data reflects reality instead of whatever a rep had time to type in — forecasts get more trustworthy.

See it against one of your own calls — a 20-minute walkthrough, recorded or live, whichever’s easier to set up.
