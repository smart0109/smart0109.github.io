The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why MedTec, why now

MedTec has spent nearly three decades building MBST into a recognized, ISO 13485-certified nuclear MR therapy, now backed by Lifco's scale since 2022 and still ranked among Germany's most innovative SMEs.

But every clinic that adopts an MBST system is its own integration: appointment, practice-management and outcomes data need to move between MedTec's systems and each clinic's own software, one clinic at a time.

That's where Vorro's pre-built connectors help: turning each new orthopedic or sports-medicine clinic onto MBST into a standard connector to its practice-management system, instead of a one-off integration for Dr. Schreiner's clinical and technical teams to manage.
