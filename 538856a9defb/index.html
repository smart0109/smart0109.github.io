A finished note only pays off once it reaches every system.

A hospital runs dozens of systems that were never built to talk to each other — the chart, billing, labs, insurers. Codes that don’t make it into all of them don’t come back at full value: they’re written off, or sold to a debt buyer for pennies on the dollar. Getting a finished, correctly coded note into every system while it’s still fresh is the only version where the hospital keeps the whole amount — and that delivery work is what Vorro removes from the critical path.
