The Integration Pitch

Built for engineers who are tired of building interfaces

Every new transplant center or cell therapy partner NMDP onboards shows up with its own systems, its own data formats, and its own integration timeline. That's the reality behind the WSO2 migration -- reconciling SOAP and REST, legacy middleware, and inconsistent security models across 446 organizations that were never built to talk to each other. It works, but it's also the kind of plumbing that eats engineering capacity a five-year modernization plan can't spare.

Vorro sits in that same layer -- as a purpose-built interoperability engine rather than a general-purpose API gateway your team has to keep extending. FHIR, HL7v2, and C-CDA in, clean normalized data out, with new partner connections measured in days instead of another custom build cycle.

For a CIO who inherited a mandate to modernize, scale, and automate, the win isn't a new dashboard -- it's engineering hours redirected from interface maintenance to the registry, matching, and cell therapy platform work that actually moves NMDP's five-year plan forward.
