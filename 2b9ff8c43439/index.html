The Integration Pitch

From single-site pilots to enterprise-wide surgical planning

Dr. Barry Rosen joined SimBioSys as Chief Medical Officer in January 2024, the same month TumorSight Viz earned its first FDA clearance — bringing 25+ years as a breast surgical oncologist and chair of the National Quality Metrics for Breast Centers to a platform that converts a routine DCE-MRI into a patient-specific 3D digital twin surgeons use to plan breast-conserving surgery. TumorSight only creates value in the OR the moment it can see the same imaging every hospital is already generating, and get its output back into the surgeon's workflow inside whatever EHR and PACS that hospital runs.

The highest-leverage moment is now: in July 2026, SimBioSys partnered with Ferrum Health to distribute TumorSight Viz through Ferrum's Model Hub, moving the platform from single-institution collaborations — Mayo Clinic's BEAUTY trial, NYU Langone Health, Baylor College of Medicine — to enterprise-wide deployment across health systems nationally. Every new health system in that rollout arrives with its own EHR, its own PACS, its own imaging and pathology stack, and TumorSight needs a repeatable way to plug into each one without a bespoke integration project per site.*

That repeatable layer is what Vorro builds. Instead of SimBioSys engineers hand-wiring HL7 feeds and FHIR endpoints for every Epic, Oracle Health, or athenahealth environment a new Ferrum Health partner happens to run, Vorro provides one integration surface — DICOM and imaging ingestion, HL7v2/FHIR R4 delivery of TumorSight's digital twin output back into the chart, C-CDA and webhook support — so each enterprise rollout looks the same to SimBioSys's product and clinical team regardless of which record sits on the other side.
