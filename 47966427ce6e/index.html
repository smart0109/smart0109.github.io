The Integration Pitch

One layer for a network that's about to get bigger

Axis Integrated Mental Health has spent the last year proving the model works: a $2.3M equity stake from TMS-device maker BrainsWay in August 2025, followed by a $1M milestone tranche completed in March 2026 after Axis hit its revenue targets ahead of schedule. That capital is earmarked for growing a four-clinic Front Range network — Denver, Aurora, Boulder, Westminster — that already runs Deep TMS, Spravato, and ketamine therapy on top of athenaOne, live since 2020 across 13 providers.

The highest-leverage moment is now: Dr. Churi joined as Chief Medical Officer in April 2026 with an explicit mandate to build reproducible clinical frameworks as Axis scales past its current footprint — the exact moment when referral intake, prior authorization, and documentation habits either get standardized once or calcify into four (soon more) different local workarounds.

Vorro gives Axis one interoperability layer that sits on top of athenaOne rather than replacing it — normalizing referrals from hospitals, EDs, and primary care into structured intake instead of fax and PDF, automating the 278 prior-auth traffic that TMS and Spravato both generate at high volume, and layering ambient documentation onto psychiatrist and PMHNP visits so clinical notes keep pace with a patient population Axis has already grown sharply in weekly intake volume. As Axis adds its fifth, sixth, and seventh location, that layer is what keeps 'reproducible' true instead of aspirational.
