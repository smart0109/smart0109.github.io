The Integration Pitch

Turning court deadlines into clinical bandwidth

Oregon State Hospital sits at the intersection of the courts and clinical care: patients arrive on Aid-and-Assist orders from county jails, on civil commitments from community mental health programs, and as Guilty Except for Insanity transfers — each with its own paperwork trail and its own clock. Federal court oversight now enforces a 7-day admission deadline, and CMS jeopardy findings in recent years made clear that record accuracy and care documentation aren't administrative footnotes here, they're the difference between a hospital in compliance and one that isn't.

The highest-leverage moment is now — with a Deputy Chief Medical Officer's clinical teams already stretched across two campuses, three legal commitment pathways, and a fixed bed count, every hour spent chasing a referral packet or re-entering a jail's intake note is an hour not spent on the treatment mall. That's exactly the seam Vorro is built to close.*

Vorro sits underneath OSH's clinical record and pulls the surrounding world into one coherent stream — county jail and court referrals, community mental health program histories, lab and pharmacy data, and OHP/Medicare eligibility — normalized into FHIR, HL7, and C-CDA so admissions teams see a complete picture the moment a patient arrives, not days into their stay. Layer in ambient documentation at the bedside and Sasha's clinicians get back the minutes the court clock keeps taking away.
