The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

Vandalia Health just finished stitching together three legacy health systems onto one Oracle Cerner instance — and simultaneously proved, with its new cardiac-alert partnership, that real-time data exchange changes clinical outcomes. That combination is rare: system-wide technical readiness paired with a CMO who has already shown he'll champion new data infrastructure.

The highest-leverage moment is now* — while Dr. Nanda's team is still hardening post-merger workflows and looking for the next win after the cardiac pilot. Vorro slots into that Cerner backbone to extend the same real-time, structured data flow beyond the cath lab: referring clinics, regional labs and imaging, payer authorization and billing, and ambient documentation that keeps clinicians out of the chart and in the room.

Dr. Nanda's own résumé — Cerner physician executive, then CMIO at two health systems, now CMO of West Virginia's largest — makes him the internal champion who can move this from pilot to platform faster than almost any peer CMO in the country.
