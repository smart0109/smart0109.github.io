The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

Innsena advises digital health companies, payers and health systems on go-to-market strategy and stakeholder engagement, each client bringing its own CRM and market data systems into the consulting relationship.

Even where each client's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same stakeholder and campaign record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a healthcare consulting firm Innsena's size spends on technology.
