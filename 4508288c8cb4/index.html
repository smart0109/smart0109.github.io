The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

TAG runs membership management, event registration, and professional society programs for 30,000 members across the state, each chapter and society bringing its own registration and communications systems into the mix.

Even where each chapter's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same member record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a nonprofit trade association TAG's size spends on technology.
