The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

CVR runs more than 120 clinics nationwide, including several recently acquired regional vein practices, each with its own scheduling, EHR and billing setup even as they fold into the CVR brand.

Even where each clinic's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same patient and revenue record as new acquisitions come online.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a 120-clinic network CVR's size spends on technology.
