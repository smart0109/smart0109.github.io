The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

Jericho Road runs primary care, maternity, dental, behavioral health, pharmacy and interpreter services across five Buffalo sites, plus clinics overseas, all serving patients who often speak more than 20 different languages.

Even where the core EHR looks unified on paper, Vorro's job is making sure dental, behavioral health, pharmacy and interpreter systems all agree on the same patient record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, taking roughly 35% out of what a health center Jericho Road's size spends on technology.
