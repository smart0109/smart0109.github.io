The Integration Pitch

For the CTO wiring 150+ collection points into one system

Every new regional lab, referring hospital, or clinic partner Dr. B. Lal signs is, underneath the marketing win, another custom interface: another HL7 feed to map, another format to normalize before it lands in the central lab system, another integration your team owns forever. That's engineering hours spent on plumbing instead of the roadmap — the AI-led diagnostics work and Health Dashboard features that actually earned Singh his CIO100 recognition.

Vorro sits as one FHIR/HL7/C-CDA layer between the central lab, the 12 regional labs, the collection network, referring providers, and the patient app — so onboarding the next hospital or clinic partner becomes a configuration exercise instead of a bespoke build, and ABHA-linked records flow out in a standard format without a one-off project each time.

That frees the team Singh built to keep shipping — ambient documentation and coding support for referring clinicians, faster longitudinal biomarker tracking in the Health Dashboard, and ABDM-aligned interoperability — instead of re-fighting the same integration problem for every center the network adds.
