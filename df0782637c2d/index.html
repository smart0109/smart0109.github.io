The Integration Pitch

Where Vorro fits

The Pointe Malibu runs lean by design: a 5:1 staff ratio, a tight clinical team spanning internal medicine, psychiatry, chronic pain, and primary therapy, and an alumni network that depends on continuity long after discharge. That model lives or dies on how fast information moves between the people delivering care — not on the size of the IT department behind it.

The highest-leverage moment is now: with a nationally distinctive clinical model (chronic pain recovery, polyvagal-informed care) to document and defend to payers and referring clinicians, every hour a clinician spends re-entering notes instead of practicing at the top of their license is an hour the program's differentiation goes unrecorded.

Vorro connects intake, medical, psychiatric, and nursing documentation with referral and alumni systems, and layers ambient scribing and real-time coding on top — so a small, high-touch team can produce the same continuity of record a large health system runs, without adding headcount.
