The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

Spartech runs manufacturing execution, quality, and supply chain systems across 14 plants serving over 1,000 customers, each plant bringing its own combination of legacy manufacturing systems into the mix following its recent ownership change.

Even where each plant's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same production and quality record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a plastics manufacturer Spartech's size spends on technology.
