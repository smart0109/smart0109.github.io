Why Vorro, why now

Fama runs AI-powered social media screening and data pipeline systems for 3,600 customers and major background screening partners, each integration bringing its own combination of HRIS and ATS systems into the platform.

Even where each partner's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same screening and candidate record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a screening technology company Fama's size spends on technology.
