The Integration Pitch

The Integration Layer Under CeeCee

CCS has already proven agentic AI works at real scale: CeeCee, built with Deloitte, is handling more than 9 in 10 inbound calls today and is on pace to autonomously process 70-80% of CCS's 100,000+ monthly intake documents by year end. That's a serious engineering win — and it also means CCS's data-ingestion surface just became mission-critical infrastructure instead of a back-office afterthought.

Every referral, order, and claim CeeCee has to reason about still has to reach it first — from a different referring physician's EHR, a fax line, a payer portal, or a device manufacturer's feed, each shaped differently, each maintained as its own one-off interface.

Vorro is a FHIR-native integration engine built for exactly that layer: replace the point-to-point HL7/fax/portal connections with one standards-based pipe, so referral intake, payer eligibility, and device orders land in a consistent structured format before CeeCee, PropheSee, or LivingConnected ever touch them — meaning more of that 30%+ opex win comes from the model, and less gets eaten by connector maintenance. Vorro also ships an ambient documentation layer (scribe, live transcription, ICD-10/CPT coding, real-time translation) that can sit directly on top of care-coaching calls, plus a standard FHIR/HL7/C-CDA/REST/webhook API for anything the team wants to build in-house.
