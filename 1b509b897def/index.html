The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why now

Opelousas General runs two hospital campuses plus more than a dozen specialty clinics and outpatient locations, from the Wound Treatment Center to the Mary Bird Perkins Cancer Center to its Women's Health and Orthopaedic clinics, each generating its own patient, scheduling and billing data.

Even where each clinic's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same patient and revenue record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a health system OGHS's size spends on technology.
