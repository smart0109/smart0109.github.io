The Integration Pitch

Why Vorro, why MiBA, why now

MiBA has built real momentum in oncology data: a joint venture between American Oncology Network and Ascend Technologies Group, now expanding through alliances with American Oncology Research, Oncology Consultants and Caris Life Sciences.

But every oncology practice and lab MiBA plugs into (AON's network, American Oncology Research, Oncology Consultants, Caris) runs its own EHR and lab system, and each one has to be extracted and normalized before MiBA's AI can find insight in it.

That's exactly where Vorro's pre-built oncology EHR and lab connectors help: turning each new practice or partner MiBA onboards into a connector, not a custom data-extraction project, so Mark's team can scale the alliance network faster.
