Vangst runs staffing, payroll, and job board systems across a rapidly growing base of 2,000+ cannabis businesses, with each recent acquisition adding its own legacy staffing systems into the mix.

Even where each acquired brand's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same worker and client record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a staffing platform Vangst's size spends on technology.
