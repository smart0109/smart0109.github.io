Why Vorro, why now

Caring Professionals coordinates home care, CDPAP and private-pay services across metropolitan New York City, each program with its own scheduling, caregiver and billing systems.

Even where each program's own systems look tidy on paper, Vorro's job is making sure every one of them agrees on the same client and caregiver record.

The payoff is concrete: one integration layer retires the redundant interfaces and manual data movement that quietly drain the budget, streamlining operations and taking roughly 35% out of what a home care agency Caring Professionals' size spends on technology.
